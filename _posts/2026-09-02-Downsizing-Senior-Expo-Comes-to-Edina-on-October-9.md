Do you or your parents know that [downsizing](https://www.johnschustergroup.com/blog/more-homes-being-listed-inventory-favoring-less-square-feet) is coming, but have no idea where to begin, who to call, or what needs to happen first? Whether you're just starting to think about the future or your family needs help right away, the DownsizingMinnesota.com Downsizing Senior Expo was created for you.

**An afternoon at Braemar on October 9.** Join us on Friday, October 9, from 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. at Braemar Golf Course in Edina. This free event brings together the professionals seniors and their adult children may need to plan a safer, smarter, and less stressful move.

**Experts for every step of the move.** You'll meet specialists in [aging in place](https://www.johnschustergroup.com/blog/to-renovate-or-move), senior living communities, estate planning, wealth management, senior insurance, and home accessibility. There are also professionals who handle organizing,[<u>packing, and moving</u>](https://www.johnschustergroup.com/blog/new-home-owners-what-to-unpack-first), estate sales, and home cleanouts, along with help [selling the family home](https://www.johnschustergroup.com/blog/preparing-to-sell-your-home-a-guide-by-the-john-schuster-group-of-coldwell-banker-realty) and much more.

{% include pullquote.html %}

**Build your plan in one place.** Instead of making dozens of phone calls and searching online without knowing whom to trust, you can meet these professionals, ask questions, compare your options, and start building a complete plan. Even if you're years away from moving, this is the perfect time to learn what your choices are. If your family needs help now, it's a chance to connect directly with the people who can help you take the next step.

**Take home a free planning guide.** Every registered attendee also receives a senior downsizing guide filled with information and planning tools you can bring home and share with your family.

Attendance is free, but space is limited, and you must register to receive your tickets. Visit [DownsizingMinnesota.com](https://downsizingminnesota.com) today to get your free tickets and give your family the information and resources they need to downsize. We hope to see you and your family there.
