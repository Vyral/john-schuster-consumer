Many Minnesota homeowners assume a winter listing automatically means accepting less money, but the market doesn’t work that way. Winter changes things, but it doesn’t automatically weaken the seller.

**Winter buyers tend to be more serious.** There are usually [fewer listings](https://www.nar.realtor/the-facts/consumer-guide-8-tips-for-selling-your-home-in-winter#:~:text=there%20are%20generally%20fewer%20homes%20on%20the%20market%20during%20this%20time) competing for attention, and buyers who are willing to tour homes in the snow and cold tend to be more serious.

**Presentation is the real winter challenge.** Snow can hide landscaping, decks, roofs, driveways, drainage, and the overall condition of the exterior. Short daylight hours can make a home feel darker, while boots, salt, wet floors, and bulky coats can make a showing feel less inviting. A seller who ignores those details may lose money, but the season itself isn’t the problem.

{% include pullquote.html %}

**Launch with a complete marketing package.** A strong winter launch starts with [a complete marketing package](https://www.johnschustergroup.com/comprehensive_marketing_plan). If possible, use professional exterior photos taken before the snow comes. Keep walkways and steps clear and well lit, and make the entry warm and uncluttered. Inside, replace any burned-out bulbs, open the window coverings, and keep the lighting consistent so the home doesn’t feel dark on short winter days. A comfortable temperature matters too, and so does making it easy for buyers to picture daily life in the home.

**Price for today’s winter competition.** [Pricing](https://www.johnschustergroup.com/selling) also needs to reflect the exact winter competition. Don’t price from a spring sale without adjusting for what buyers can choose from today. At the same time, don’t discount the home simply because it’s winter.

The best season to sell is the one when your home is [prepared](https://www.johnschustergroup.com/blog/preparing-to-sell-your-home-a-guide-by-the-john-schuster-group-of-coldwell-banker-realty), the competition is understood, and the move works for your life. Here in Minnesota, a well-planned winter listing can be an advantage.
