If your home is still on the market as Thanksgiving gets closer, you’ve probably wondered whether to pull it and try again in January. Sometimes that’s the right call. More often, taking a listing down just because the holidays arrived costs sellers their best opportunity.

**Holiday buyers usually have a deadline.** Fewer buyers are out in late November and December, but [holiday buyers tend to be more serious](https://www.hgtv.com/lifestyle/real-estate/top-10-tips-for-selling-your-home-during-the-holidays#:~:text=holiday%20buyers%20tend%20to%20be%20more%20serious), and many are highly motivated. [A job change](https://www.johnschustergroup.com/relocation-services), a lease that’s ending, a family move, or a financial reason to close usually drives them to book showings this time of year.

If your home is the right fit for someone who has to move now, pulling it off the market means that buyer never gets the chance to find it.

**Fewer listings help yours stand out.** Plenty of sellers step away for the holidays, so the competition thins out. A [well-presented home](https://www.johnschustergroup.com/blog/preparing-to-sell-your-home-a-guide-by-the-john-schuster-group-of-coldwell-banker-realty) gets noticed quickly when buyers don’t have as many options to compare it against.

{% include pullquote.html %}

**Staying active requires the right positioning.** Keeping the home listed only helps when the listing is set up correctly. A price that’s no longer competitive or photos that feel stale can work against you. Showings that are hard to schedule, or negative market history the home has picked up, can do the same.

**Three questions should guide the decision.** First, are serious buyers still shopping in your price range? Second, is your current price supported by [the newest comparable sales](https://www.johnschustergroup.com/selling) and the competition you’re up against? Third, can you keep the home show-ready without ruining the holidays?

**The calendar alone shouldn’t decide it.** For some sellers, the best plan is to stay active with controlled showing windows. Others are better off pausing briefly to [improve the presentation](https://www.johnschustergroup.com/comprehensive_marketing_plan) and relaunching with a clear January strategy. Withdrawing automatically just because the calendar turned is the one move I’d avoid.
