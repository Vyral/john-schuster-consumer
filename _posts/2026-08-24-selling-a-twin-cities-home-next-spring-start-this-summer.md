If you're thinking about selling your home next spring, the best thing you can do is take action now. Every summer, we meet with homeowners who are considering a move the following year, and we build a custom game plan well before their home ever hits the market.

**Summer photos capture your home at its best.** Part of that plan is taking professional exterior photos while your landscape is looking its absolute best. When your home lists next spring, buyers get to see your lush grass, mature trees, flowers, and full [curb appeal](https://www.nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/research-reports/remodeling-impact-report-outdoor-features) instead of leftover snow and a brown lawn. That first impression is hard to recreate once the season turns, which is exactly why capturing it in summer matters.
